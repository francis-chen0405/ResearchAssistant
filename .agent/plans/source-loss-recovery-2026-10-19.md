## Implementation

- Fresh Round 1 assesses up to four promising retained sources before Gap Analysis. Its
  bounded queue leaves 120,000 tokens and half the remaining cost for subsequent work.
  Smaller budgets can admit fewer or no checkpoint sources. The frozen checkpoint plan
  and source checkpoints support replay; final analysis reuses the assessments and admits
  evidence with the final selection provenance. Existing assessments can finalize even if
  later searches leave no budget for a new analysis queue. No early judgment is automatically
  a citation. Preliminary plans have their own artifact type so source-history inspection
  cannot mistake them for final selection.
- Typed supported propositions, relationships, limitations, and failures enter Gap Analysis
  and subsequent adaptive input. Guidance explicitly separates readable-source counts from
  evidence sufficiency and asks for the exact missing claim component and targeted search.
- Ranked acquisition tries later eligible candidates after inaccessible or unusable captures,
  until its per-direction usable target, configured acquisition bound, or hard 60-cluster
  ceiling. Alternate-location fallback, fair ordering, cancellation, and audits remain active.
- Extraction sees at most 8,000 UTF-8 bytes of exact numbered sentences, prioritizing Probe
  passages and nearby context. Original sentence IDs and snapshot offsets remain authoritative;
  selecting an omitted sentence is rejected. Fresh sentence-selection output is capped at
  8,192 tokens or the lower configured route limit, consistently in transport and reservations.
  Unknown usage still retains its actual conservative reservation.
  Provider-native requests expose the numbered view once; the complete snapshot stays local
  for validation. Shared guidance reaches both generic rendered and native provider prompts.
  Queue planning keeps conservative whole-snapshot estimates for downstream Analyst work.
- Initial planning may consider up to twelve deduplicated related paper titles/URLs from
  the twenty latest saved final outputs in the same database. This read-only lookup freezes
  metadata and originating run/source IDs for replay. Prior quotes, scores, judgments, and
  citations are not imported; chosen leads require fresh discovery and assessment.
- Discovery diagnostics summarize capped, failed and retained sources. Existing source-lead
  inspection retains individual stage dispositions. Result cards label unassessed recommendations
  provisional and disclose assessed relationships and strong/partial/weak claim fit.
