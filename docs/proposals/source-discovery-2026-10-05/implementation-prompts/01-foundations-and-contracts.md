## 1. Map ownership and freeze a concrete specification

Trace initial planning and Round-1 execution in `agents/v2_initial_planner.py` and `researchassistant/research/v2_orchestrator.py`, adaptive execution in `agents/v2_adaptive_search.py`, discovery/Scout/clustering in `agents/v2_discovery.py`, acquisition/Probe in `agents/v2_acquisition.py`, selection in `agents/v2_source_selection.py`, provider adapters, source/trail/history readers, and API/CLI/desktop configuration. Confirm how `ResearchControls.sources_per_stance_per_round` currently works; do not repurpose it to mean metadata result depth.

Define one fresh discovery policy and explicit version identities shared by later phases. Keep historical policies supported. Initial implementation defaults should target 20 metadata results per logical query, maximum configurable 50; at most three physical pages per logical operation; 300 retained raw candidates per round and 1,000 per run; at most 60 candidates considered by Scout per round, further limited by safely available model budget; and the established acquisition cap of at most 25 clusters per round. For expansion, default to at most three seeds per run, one hop, ten unique neighbors per seed across relationship types, and thirty unique expansion candidates per run. These are independent ceilings, not allocations or guaranteed minima. Provider restrictions and smaller remaining budgets take precedence. Define a deterministic fair allocation for enabled directions/providers instead of exhausting the whole cap on whichever response finishes first. Tighten defaults if code-level budget analysis requires it, recording the reason and testing the result; do not loosen hard limits or misstate effective depth.

## 2. Typed representations required by all phases

Add narrowly owned, immutable, strict Pydantic contracts, reusing compatible existing ones, for:

- Discovery policy and per-provider capabilities, including search modes, field/operator support, metadata limits, pagination, identity lookup, references/citing/related-work support, and unsupported features.
- A conceptual query specification with required concept groups, bounded synonyms, optional methods/outcomes, purpose, enabled direction/provider, round, target Gap IDs, and application-owned identity. Keep the exact user claim separate.
- A compiled provider query/action: actual query text and sanitized parameters, mode, requested/effective depth, logical operation ID, compiler/policy identity, and fingerprint. Query actions and graph-neighbor actions must have distinct types; do not disguise graph requests as arbitrary search text.
- Provider attempt/page records: parent operation, sequence, bounds, status, response/result identity or hash, counts, timing where available, known/unknown cost basis, and typed failure. Credentials and credential-bearing URLs are never serialized.
- Raw and normalized discovery candidates; stable identity/grouping; explicit dispositions for duplicate, excluded, cap/budget prevented, not scouted, shortlisted, fetched, and unavailable cases; rank components and rationale; retained source locations without weakening current same-work identity rules.
- Preview requests/results: exact claim and permitted lane, snapshot ID/hash, exact offsets/text, section/context, relevance signals, content completeness/unknown classification, and versioned policy.
- Seed eligibility, work resolution, expansion actions/results/edges, pending or skipped outcomes, and provenance linking an expanded candidate to the seed, relationship, provider, round, and gaps.

Every accepted/failed record must bind to its run and application-owned identity. Specify meaningful unknown values instead of inventing quality, study design, full-text availability, edge verification, or independence. Reject contradictory caps, empty required concepts, invalid modes, malformed IDs, stale hashes, cross-run ownership, cycles where prohibited, and conflicting completed replays. Keep provider relevance scores separate; they are not comparable across providers by assumption.

With no provider idempotency/recovery guarantee, a restart must not blindly repeat an unknown request or claim no charge; retain exposure, show an explicit incomplete outcome, and require a new separately reserved attempt if recovery policy permits one.

Bind the policy, capabilities/compiler versions, candidate-ranking/preview/seed versions, effective settings, changed prompt schemas, and provider configuration to fresh run fingerprints and packaged source identity. Keep old-client handling explicit: distinguish missing historical fields from selected new settings. Do not deserialize unknown policy identities through permissive current defaults.

Protect downstream model capacity before admitting more Scout, search-planning, or selection work. Graph metadata calls and search pages consume the applicable provider request/cost budgets. New audit counters distinguish logical queries, HTTP requests, metadata records, unique work candidates, acquisition attempts, usable snapshots, and admitted evidence. Atomic reservation and ownership must survive concurrent operations and cancellation. Do not store unrelated runs in an unbounded process cache.
